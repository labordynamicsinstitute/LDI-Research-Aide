## Restricted Deposit

When the open ICPSR deposit is labelled "Restricted"

Subject Line:

> Action Needed for your open ICPSR Deposit (AEAREP-xxx)


> Hi Dr. [Author],
> We're reviewing your open ICPSR deposit for "[Title]" (MC Number) and noticed you answered "Yes" for the submission questions.
Please answer these questions in regards to the data in the deposit you are submitting. The answer should not consider any other data that may have been used as part of the manuscript’s analysis, but that are not present in this particular deposit. Therefore the answer to the first two questions should be "No". Please re-submit with the accurate answers and delete any restricted access data so the deposit is ready for publication.
>
> More information in the link below.
Instructions on submitting to Open ICPSR: https://aeadataeditor.github.io/aea-de-guidance/data-deposit-aea.html#submission-questions
I hope this helps and please let us know if you have any questions. Thank you.

> Request for Restricted Access Data (AEAREP-xxx)

Send it to authors, cc to `dataeditor-queue@aeapubs.org` and possibly `dataeditor@aeapubs.org`.

> Dr. *Author*,
> 
> We received a request to conduct the AEA reproducibility check for your manuscript entitled "X." On the Data and Code Availability form which you submitted it was indicated that the data can be made available to the Data Editor privately. Would it be possible for you to provide the restricted-access data so that we may proceed to verify the reproducibility of the entire replication package? The data would not be published, and would be deleted by us once we have completed our process. Our preferred sharing method is a Box File Request (specific to your manuscript), the link for which can be found below, but can alternatively happen via Dropbox or similar means, and should be compliant with your data use agreement.
