## Update Codeocean Capsule Metadata with Link to the Article DOI

- Obtain the information on Codeocean capsule number (the numeric part of the published Codeocean DOI), and of the linked article (from the article landing page).
- Send an email to support@codeocean.com

```
Request to Link DOI to Capsule Metadata
```

```
Hello,

I hope this email finds you well. I work for the American Economic Association's Data Editor. I am writing to request an update to the metadata for a capsule linked to one of our articles. The capsule was previously published. The associated article has now also been published. The Codeocean capsule shows the linked publication as "yet to be published". Could you add the article DOI to the capsule metadata, please?

The Capsule number is: (insert capsule number)

The DOI of the related publication should be updated to
(insert DOI from article page)

The full article citation linked to this capsule is
(insert citation from article page)

Please let me know if you need any additional information to process this request.

Thank you for your assistance.
```
- Once you receive confirmation from Code Ocean Support and you checked that the DOI has been added, transition the ticket to "Done".
