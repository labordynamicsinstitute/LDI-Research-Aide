## Compliance

- [ ] Check training on [Cornell Research Services](https://researchservices.cornell.edu/training). At a minimum,
  - [ ] CITI Course for COI
  - [ ] CITI Course for IRB (note: most of what we do at the Replication Lab is not subject to IRB. After taking the course, do you know why?)
  - [ ] CITI Course for RCR
  - [ ] May need to also do "[Attestation for Policy 4.21 Research Data Retention and Policy 4.22 Export and Import Control Compliance](https://researchservices.cornell.edu/training/attestation-policy-421-research-data-retention-and-policy-422-export-and-import-control)"
- [ ] Conflict of Interest forms
  - [ ] You will need to fill out the [RASS-COI](https://rass.cornell.edu/login) form.

## Software 

- [ ] Check training for Stata and R at [CCSS](https://socialsciences.cornell.edu/computing-and-data/workshops-and-training). If none are listed, inquire.
- [ ] Check for BioHPC/Linux training at the [BioHPC webpage](https://biohpc.cornell.edu/workshops.aspx).
- [ ] Optional, but possibly (more) useful:
  - [ ] Do the [Carpentries' Shell lesson](https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice)

#### Jira admin account 

- Login as admin (dataeditor@aeapubs.org). Go to <https://id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security>.
- Select "Two-step verification".
- **Disable two-step verification** (only way to switch keys).
- **Set up two-step verification**
- Screenshot the QR code and share with RA, then delete the QR code from the relevant sharing app!
