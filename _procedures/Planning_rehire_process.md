# Re-Hiring

## Adjusting the Forms

- Forms should be in [Pre-Publication](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/1/folders/1uhZHu0MOJIth-4oDc44XkUdHS7ncMqpj) folder on Google Drive.
- Key form is "LDI Replication Lab [Summer](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1lTMzBDPwNTWi8Hkuw2wkja8bYf7EWKKKoOApiFs89Ac/edit?usp=drive_web)/[Spring](https://docs.google.com/forms/u/1/d/1zzn5bgV0MYqUdK7PgFm_3YP2ePmI559LcL5JnAqvT3g/edit?usp=drive_web)/[Fall](https://docs.google.com/forms/u/1/d/1Rhwv1efD13u1_RvsT_cLO69njpdY4b42fVcLi8xrdo4/edit?usp=drive_web) Planning".
- All responses should be captured in the "[LDI Replication Lab Planning (Responses)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OIo-ZjPgaXd1qqmk-urgmyv2W77pYLc62GPFIk-sdXo/edit?gid=845847607#gid=845847607)" Google Sheet (see below)

## Archiving

- Make a copy of the previous state of the form ("Copy of LDI..."), deactivate responses,  and move it to the "Obsolete" folder.
- All other steps here happen in the MAIN form, not the copy!
