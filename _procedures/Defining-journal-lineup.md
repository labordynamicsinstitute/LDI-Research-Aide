The editorial staff will send an email about the line-up of upcoming journal issues. They're usually titled something like "[Journal] [Month] Lineup".

Example:

![Email Ex]({{ '/images/lineupEmail.png' | relative_url }})

1. Open the attached xlsx file.
2. Search the Manuscript Central number on Jira

![Xlsx Ex]({{ '/images/lineupExcel.png' | relative_url }})

3. Go to the most recent Jira Task ticket associated with the manuscript.

4. Go to the "Other Links" tab of the ticket and scroll down to *Journal Month* and *Journal Year* and update the fields based on the Excel title.

![Jira Ex]({{ '/images/lineupJira.png' | relative_url }})

5. Update the [Forthcoming Publications filter](https://aeadataeditors.atlassian.net/issues/?filter=10036) with the latest month and year. Check that the issues you just marked show up.
