Current Research Aide [RA] and  Data Editor [DE] onboards incoming replacement [R]. Tags identify who can do what. 

## Prep

- [R] obtains NetID
- [R] activates LastPass
- [R] activates [Google Drive account for Cornell](https://it.cornell.edu/gsuite)

## Accounts

### Core

- [RA, DE] Invite to Jira and Bitbucket (see general manual about process)
- [RA, DE] Invite to CCSS Cloud (send email to [ccss-researchsupport@cornell.edu](mailto:ccss-researchsupport@cornell.edu?Subject="New CCSS Cloud user for Lars Group") specifying the NetID.
- [RA, DE] Request access to the L: drive on non-Cloud systems (may not be necessary)
  
### Communications

- [DE] shares password to `dataeditor@aeapubs.org` and `dataeditor-queue@aeapubs.org` email account via **LastPass**. Access is via [https://www.vilhuber.com/aea/](https://www.vilhuber.com/aea/)
- [DE] Add to `ldilab@cornell.edu` and `ldi@cornell.edu` ([Cornell EGA management](https://it.cornell.edu/resource-accounts/find-resource-accounts-exchange-account-manager))
  - `ldilab`: "SendAs" and "Access" permissions
  - `ldi`: "Access" only permissions
- [RA, DE] Add to `LDI-Lab-l` listserv at [https://www.list.cornell.edu/](https://www.list.cornell.edu/)
- [DE]  Add [R] as co-host for the [ldi@cornell.edu Zoom account](https://zoom.us/) (Note: not a regular Cornell zoom account)
- [RA, DE] Add [R] to Slack channel (deactivate upon exit)
